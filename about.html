About me

Why I chose IT, and where I'm headed

I got into Information Technology because I've always liked understanding how things work under the hood — whether that's an application, a database, or the network carrying data between them. IT felt like the field where that curiosity could actually turn into a career, and one where there's always something new to learn.

My career goal is to eventually run my own business in cybersecurity or network engineering. I like the idea of building something of my own rather than just working within someone else's system, though I know that takes time and experience to get right. If that path doesn't work out the way I'm hoping, I'd be glad to build a career as a network engineer instead — it's a field I genuinely enjoy, not just a fallback.

Within IT, I'm most drawn to networking and security — the infrastructure side of things that most people never see but that everything else depends on. I'm also developing my technical writing, since being able to explain a system clearly is just as important as building it.
